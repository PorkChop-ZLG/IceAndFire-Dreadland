Ice And Fire: Dread Land

Welcome to Ice And Fire: Dread Land — a NeoForge addon for Ice And Fire: Community Edition. The Dreadland here isn't just a frozen wasteland; it's a brand-new adventure dimension made up of four realms: Iceland, Fireland, Lightning, and Dreadland.

⚠️ Development Status: Early Alpha

A large amount of content is still unfinished. The Dread Queen boss fight, other bosses, and creatures are not complete yet. The currently playable main quest line ends at the Dreadland; more content is still in development.

✨ Features

  • A brand-new Dreadland dimension: Explore four realms — Iceland, Fireland, Lightning, and Dreadland — each with its own terrain, structures, and creatures.
  • Three churches and dungeons: Find the Ice Church, Fire Church, and Lightning Church, fend off Dread creatures, and descend into the dungeons beneath the churches to challenge dragons.
  • Four keys: Iceland Key, Fireland Key, Lightning Key, and Dreadland Key. Collect all three realm keys and combine them into the Dreadland Key.
  • Dread Portal: Build a 5×7 frame using Dreadstone Bricks, then right-click the frame with the Dreadland Key to activate the Dread Portal.
  • Lightning realm mechanics: The Lightning realm naturally generates spectacular thunderstorms.

🎮 How to Play

  1. Find the Ice Church / Fire Church / Lightning Church.
  2. Help the churches fend off incoming Dread creatures and collect Dread Shards.
  3. Trade Dread Shards to the church pastors for a Gravemaster's Key.
  4. Dig out the block in the center of the church floor's cross to enter the dungeon.
  5. Use the Gravemaster's Key to open the dungeon door and challenge the dragons guarding the keys: Ice Dragon / Fire Dragon / Lightning Dragon, earning the Iceland Key / Fireland Key / Lightning Key respectively.
  6. Combine the three keys to craft the Dreadland Key.
  7. Travel to the Mausoleum from the Ice and Fire mod's vanilla structures. Use the Gravemaster's Key to open its gate, delve deep into the Mausoleum, and find the Dread Portal Frame at its deepest point.
  8. Right-click the frame while holding the Dreadland Key to activate the portal and enter the Dreadland.

The current main quest line ends here; more content is still in development.

🌍 The Dreadland Dimension

This is a Dreadland unlike the original Ice and Fire setting:

  • Iceland: Frozen plains and forests, cold and silent.
  • Fireland: Scorched, cracked earth covered with lava and fire.
  • Lightning: A thunderstorm-ravaged realm where lightning strikes frequently.
  • Dreadland: An eerie, ominous core overgrown with Dreadwood — the final destination of your adventure.

Each realm has its own terrain, structures, and creatures, and each one has a boss waiting to be challenged (note: bosses are not complete yet). Among the four bosses, the final boss, the Dread Queen, and her mount, Black Frost, are confirmed to be completed; the other three bosses have not yet begun design.

📦 Installation

  1. Install Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge.
  2. Download Ice And Fire: Community Edition, Jupiter, and this mod, then place them in the mods folder.
  3. Launch the game and you're ready to go.
